Features

Everything your counter needs.
Nothing it doesn't.

Six connected tools that share the same data — record a sale once and your stock, dues and reports all update.

Sales Management

Bill customers in seconds with automatic discount, tax and total calculations.

  • Search and filter every sale
  • Cash, UPI, card or credit

Inventory Management

Track stock, cost and selling price for every product, with margins built in.

  • Automatic low-stock alerts
  • Stock updates on every sale

Customer Management

Keep contact details, purchase history and outstanding dues for every regular.

  • Full purchase history
  • Dues at a glance

Invoice Generation

Create clean, multi-item invoices with your business details — ready to print.

  • Print or download instantly
  • Partial payments tracked

Expense Tracking

Log rent, salaries, bills and supplier payments to see where money goes.

  • Eight ready categories
  • Monthly totals & filters

Business Analytics

Revenue, expenses and profit over any date range, plus best sellers and top customers.

  • Interactive charts
  • One-click CSV export

About

Business software that respects small businesses

LocalBiz OS is a concept product: a frontend demonstration of what a modern operating system for neighbourhood businesses could look like.

Our goal

Replace paper registers and scattered spreadsheets with one calm, focused workspace.

Built for India first

INR by default, GST-style tax, UPI and credit sales — with USD and EUR display too.

Private by design

In this demo, every record stays in your own browser. Nothing is sent to a server.

Open web tech

Plain HTML, CSS, Bootstrap 5 and JavaScript — fast, lightweight and easy to run anywhere.

Privacy

LocalBiz OS is a demonstration project with no backend.

  • Everything you enter is saved only in your browser's localStorage.
  • No data is transmitted, sold or shared.
  • Passwords are validated in the browser and never stored.
  • Clearing site data, or using Settings → Data, removes your records.

Fonts, icons and libraries are loaded from public CDNs.
